# Act 2: precision redesign

## Scope and progression

24 of 39 rooms have geometry changes: four garden rooms (3, 4, 5, 7), all seventeen castle rooms, and the three-room finale (16–18). The other fifteen retain their existing story, teaching, or advanced-mechanic purpose. The source is `ldtk/hooshang_act2.ldtk`; packed scenes are generated by the importer. The level redesign preserves movement, abilities, story, and existing gameplay art. The subsequent requested child-dialogue update is documented separately in `ACT2_DIALOGUE.md`.

The opening remains a childhood arrival and lesson. Rooms 3–7 now demand more deliberate placement before the existing advanced garden sequences. Room 8 is a breathing space; 9–13 develop spring-apex dashes, carpet remounts, reversals, and restricted headroom. The castle changes from empty shells into four short thematic loops. Red is the suggested first loop, green adds walls, blue changes pace with swimming, and gold tests commitment on crumbling supports. These wings remain freely selectable: their difficulty order is a recommendation, not a new lock. The rescue is followed by three compact synthesis rooms with interior checkpoints, then the existing story ending.

## Fairness and retries

The design uses the actual child controller: 67.5 px/s run speed, approximately 40.8 px running held-jump height (32.2 px standing), 69.8 px running jump reach, and approximately 67.8 px spring height. Input tests use normal acceleration, braking, dash cooldowns, hazards, and swimming. Most castle catches are 32 px wide against a 9 px body; the existing doorway catches remain 16 px wide. Normal land-mantle assistance is disabled only in acceptance tests to avoid depending on an optional assist.

Castle rooms are single-screen sequences with room-entry retries. The hub is a safe navigation space. The finale checkpoints are on the middle solid landings in 16 and 17 and the middle bank in 18. No paired doorway is narrowed or relocated. Castle terrain and hazard cells are checked against visible imported art; the existing watercolor assets are reused.

## Editing

Close LDtk and quit the Godot editor normally before editing/importing. `tools/rework_act2_precision.py` is the explicit one-time construction recipe, based on the immutable snapshots in `output/act2_redesign/`. Do not rerun it over later hand-edited LDtk work without merging that work first. `tools/validate_act2_precision.py` checks schema, identities, paired apertures, clear threshold interiors, and visible semantic geometry. Reimport using Godot; never hand-edit packed room scenes.

## Verification and remaining playtesting

Verified in Godot 4.6.2. Tests below use ordinary timing except the explicitly noted final mission-driver check:

- **35 castle traversal trials:** forward/return paths, four wall jumps, and both directions through the water routes; zero deaths and zero failures. These isolate a room by disabling portals and placing the player only at its starting landing.
- **Complete castle mission:** all sixteen challenge rooms, four keys collected by touch, rescue/reunion, and real courtyard exit to room 16, with zero deaths and no fixture teleports or key grants. Ordinary-timing runs reached this endpoint; the final corrected landing assertion was checked in a full `--fixed-fps 60` run (`mission_final.log`). That flag accelerates wall-clock execution with a fixed simulation delta; it is not a human speedrun measurement.
- **Connected garden run:** eleven rooms (3–13), both approach encounters, castle entry, courtyard return, and return respawns; zero unplanned deaths. The two counted deaths are deliberate retry checks.
- **All 40 directed castle openings:** real crossing followed by idle and death/respawn checks; zero failures.
- **Eight hub destinations:** reached from the entrance through real movement. The central stairwell also allows descent between upper and lower wings.
- **Connected finale 16–19:** zero deaths. A separate run deliberately dies at all three interior checkpoints and continues successfully, with exactly three deaths.
- **Optional qanat rewards:** both lemons reached through swimming detours and banked on dry land; zero deaths.
- **Shortcut probes:** 19 rooms × three policies (16 castle challenges and all three finale rooms). Holding forward clears none. Repeated jump plus flat dash clears R01, R04, and Y01, retained as valid early/rhythm alternatives. These probes are not an exhaustive search for exploits.
- **Source/import checks:** official LDtk schema, all 39 identities, all 20 paired mouths, clear threshold interiors, visible semantic geometry, and cross-project import isolation pass. The rendered material test passes for all 908 imported terrain tiles.
- **Required regression suite:** 32 of 37 pass. The five outstanding results are listed below; this is not a fully green repository.
- **Additional Act 2 regressions:** expansion, hazards, progression, quest, carpet lesson, ending save, cage reunion, castle connections, childhood tempo, and world movement isolation pass.

Evidence is under `output/act2_redesign/`: `route_results_full.json`, `mission_final.log`, `mission_results.json`, `garden_connected_latest.log`, `portals_final.log`, `hub_final.log`, `captures_finale.log`, `finale_retries.log`, `final_rewards.log`, `shortcut_results.json`, `final_shortcut_results.json`, `source_validation.json`, `import_isolation.log`, `materials.log`, and the `regressions/` and `act2_regressions*/` logs. ### Human playtesting

Input-driven success establishes that the tested solutions work; it does not establish that a human finds them fun, readable, or appropriately difficult. Human testing should focus on first-attempt route recognition, controller comfort, checkpoint pacing, optional reward value, and the difficulty jump from garden to freely selectable castle wings.
