# Darkshang escape routes — current design

Level_15–24 are long pursuit courses: 1,728–2,304 pixels wide (5.4–7.2 screens), 384 pixels high, and 11–15 ladders per room. Alternating scaffold heights combine ladder transfers, drops and optional jump/dash shortcuts. Under-deck walls prevent simply running beneath the entire course. Recovery floors contain no spikes. Checkpoints break up the long runs. Ambient brightness matches Level_1. The earlier Level_V10–V14 rooms, the Level_14 reveal, and Level_25 finale are preserved.

Level_18 and Level_24 use musical gates. Touch their five colored note tiles in pitch order while running the route; an incorrect note resets that room's melody. Their gates cover only the 32×32 exit opening and show the completed/required count. The other eight rooms have no puzzle lock. Unlocking a gate grants the normal short-lived glow, releases the doorway light, and does not stop Darkshang or cancel his attacks.

The real Darkshang actor follows the player's recorded route, including ladder climbs and jumps. The previous room logic that parked him and substituted a separate attack figure is removed for these courses. His original animated body remains at a consistent 0.9 scale. Contact plays the existing absorption animation and resolves through one normal death and checkpoint respawn. The first 16 pixels after entry/respawn still provide a safe commitment threshold.

Pursuit delay tightens from 1.8 to 1.575 seconds across the escape. Telegraphing a lunge takes 0.7 seconds; a lunge moves at 170–197 pixels/second. Lunges are scheduled every 9–7.2 seconds when there is enough separation, and are not initiated while the player is climbing. He continues ordinary pursuit throughout projectile warnings. Clouds move at 138–174 pixels/second until solid geometry or the room edge stops them. Later rooms mix clouds and location-locked marks. Projectile attacks repeat every 2.7–2.07 seconds after release, with a 1.05-second warning and at most two active clouds. Clouds cannot interrupt absorption's locked animation.

Data: resources/levels/act1_expansion.json. Rebuild with tools/build_act1_expansion.py --install --rooms followed by the explicit Level_15 through Level_24 names. Close Godot and LDtk before edits/import. The escape rooms occupy separate non-overlapping LDtk coordinates to accommodate their new dimensions; room identifiers still determine play order.

Tests: darkshang_chase_test checks the real actor, exactly two musical gates, five-note sequences, absorption/death/reset and continued pursuit after unlocking. darkshang_chase_playthrough uses actual movement input from entrance to exit with all threats active; it includes timing variants/retries rather than disabling attacks. Existing cloud tests cover extended travel and swept wall collisions in both directions. Earlier-room tests retain coverage for Level_V10–V14.
